# Direction: Quiet

The brief after the first round: **calm, simple, humble, elegant — none of it in an
extreme.** The three earlier directions were too loud. Quiet replaces them on the homepage.

## World

A personal letter, set like a small book. One column, a wide margin for the portrait, and
room between sections. No cards, no color blocks, no charts, no slogans.

## Palette

| Token  | Value     | Use                       |
| ------ | --------- | ------------------------- |
| Paper  | `#F8F7F3` | Page ground               |
| Ink    | `#1C1C18` | Body and headings         |
| Muted  | `#6B6961` | Secondary text            |
| Rule   | `#DEDBD2` | Section and row hairlines |
| Accent | `#5C6A55` | Links, emphasis, drop cap |

One accent, used at small scale. Nothing is highlighted with a filled background.

## Type

- **Source Serif 4** for the headline, section titles, work titles, and prose.
- **Instrument Sans** for navigation, dates, labels, and small meta. It never exceeds 12.5px
  except the hero links.
- Body: 16.5–17px, line-height 1.7–1.8, measure 54–68 characters.
- Headline: 42px at desktop, 26px on mobile. Section titles: 24px.
- Emphasis is italic moss, never bold or colored blocks.

## Layout

- 1080px page, 40px gutters, 64px header.
- Hero: text column + 240px portrait. The portrait is the only image on the page.
- Sections: 60px above and 64px below, separated by a 1px rule.
- Work and experience rows: a date column, a content column, and a small type label.
- `Details` disclosures hold the extra CV bullets. The page stays quiet until a reader asks
  for more.
- Mobile: one column, 20px gutters, period above the role, portrait at 180px.

## Signature

The drop cap on the About paragraph, the rhythm of hairlines, and a small cup of tea under
the portrait. The cup is the only ornament. Its steam is the only ambient motion.

## Why it fits

- **Calm:** one column, one image, one accent. Motion is small and slow: a hero rise, section
  reveals, a click ripple, and the cup steam.
- **Humble:** modest type sizes, no metrics band, no giant name, no motion for its own sake.
- **Elegant:** the serif/italic pairing, the tabular dates, the 1px rules, the drop cap.
- **Not extreme:** warm stone paper instead of stark white or black; moss instead of a
  saturated accent.

## Risks

- Without strong copy, restraint can read as empty. The CV text carries the page.
- The page is long because it is complete. The disclosures keep the visual weight down, but
  the scroll is still about six screens on mobile.
- The portrait is a color photo on warm paper. If it feels too present, a softer crop or a
  monochrome treatment is the next dial.
- The motion is deliberately quiet. A visitor may not notice the cup or the ripple on the
  first visit. That is the intended level.
